01 This site

A workshop with
the door open

This isn't a portfolio. It's my playground.

I use this site to build personal projects, test new AI models, and rebuild things that already worked just to see whether they can work better. It is the place where I try things before I know whether they're any good.

So treat it as permanently unfinished. Pages get added, rewritten, and torn out. Copy changes. Whole sections appear and disappear. If you land on something half-built, you haven't caught me out — you've found the point of the whole exercise.

The companies below are real and the trails are real. Everything around them is a work in progress, on purpose.

There's a second reason it exists. Most of what the internet knows about any of us was written by someone else, and language models train on that — when the record is thin, they fill the gap with something plausible. So this is also an exercise in holding the pen: first-party, current, and mine. Some of it is open to crawlers on purpose. Most of it isn't.

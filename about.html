01 Background

The long
version

Entrepreneur, adventurer, and builder, based in the Northeast.

I run multiple businesses under Canopy Holdings — design work at Sense Designs, logistics at Shipping Pilot and Fulfill, and the Crate family of gifting brands. The first two entities were filed in 2017, on the same day, and everything since has been built on that footing.

When I'm not building companies, I'm planning the next expedition — Everest Base Camp, Patagonia's W Trek, or whatever the next long walk turns out to be.

A fuller bio is coming. Like most things here, this section is a draft.

02 This site

Permanently
unfinished

This site is my playground, not my portfolio.

It's where I build personal projects, test new AI models, and rebuild things that already worked just to find out whether they can work better. Half of what's here exists because I wanted to see whether it could be done, not because anyone asked for it.

That means it's in constant flux. Pages get added, rewritten, and deleted. Copy changes. Designs get thrown out and replaced wholesale. If you come back next month, expect it to look different — and if you find something obviously half-built, you've found the point rather than a mistake.

The companies are real. The trails are real. Everything wrapped around them is an experiment in progress.

Always in flux

03 Values

What guides the work
and the adventures

  • Build things that matter. Whether it's a business, a trail plan, or a fort in the woods — make it count.
  • Earn it. No shortcuts on the mountain or in the office. The hard way is usually the right way.
  • Show up for your people. Family, team, partners — consistency and presence over grand gestures.
  • Stay curious. New trails, new markets, new skills. The day you stop learning is the day you start coasting.

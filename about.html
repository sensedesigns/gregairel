The Long Version

Greg Airel is an entrepreneur, adventurer, and builder based in the Northeast. He runs multiple businesses under Canopy Holdings, from design work at Sense Designs to logistics operations at Shipping Pilot.

When he's not building companies, he's planning the next expedition — whether that's trekking to Everest Base Camp, navigating Patagonia's W Trek, or training for a sprint triathlon.

Values

What guides the work and the adventures.

  • Build things that matter. Whether it's a business, a trail plan, or a fort in the woods — make it count.
  • Earn it. No shortcuts on the mountain or in the office. The hard way is usually the right way.
  • Show up for your people. Family, team, partners — consistency and presence over grand gestures.
  • Stay curious. New trails, new markets, new skills. The day you stop learning is the day you start coasting.

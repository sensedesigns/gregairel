03 Rules of the mountain

How this
actually works

  • Hints are free. Three per camp, a few minutes apart. They show up on your certificate and disqualify you from nothing.
  • You can always get out. Any camp will just tell you the answer. You drop off the leaderboard and keep climbing.
  • Rest between camps. The mountain holds you a short while at each one. Read the page while you wait — that is usually where the next clue is.
  • The prize is a draw, not a race. Finish within 48 hours and you are in it. No cap, no advantage to being quick.
  • The leaderboard is optional. Finish, claim a reward, stay off it entirely.

Nothing is gained by automating this. The draw is random and the rests are fixed.

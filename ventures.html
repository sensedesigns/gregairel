Every venture
earns its place

Every venture starts with the same question: what problem needs solving, and can we do it better?

No venture exists for vanity. Each one earns its place by delivering real value — to customers, to partners, and to the team. The ones that stop doing that don't stay.

The Crate brands came out of a single idea repeated in four directions: that a well-assembled box, sent at the right moment, does something a text message can't. Canopy exists to give all four the same operational backbone.
